Our Heritage

Bringing South Indian Culture To Your Plate

Preserving Decades of Tradition

Founded in 1998, Anna Cafe started as a tiny Kaapi stall serving early morning commuters with authentic degree filter coffee in traditional brass davarah cups.

Every single dish in our kitchen follows ancestral recipes—from our slow-simmered aromatic Sambar infused with home-ground spices, to naturally fermented crispy Dosas cooked on cast-iron griddles.

When you walk into Anna Cafe, you aren't just a customer; you are family ("Anna" means Elder Brother). Sit back, sip your Kaapi, and relish the timeless taste of the South.
